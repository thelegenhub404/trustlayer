Publish your agent in 5 minutes

1. Generate your keys

pip install trustlayer
tl keygen analytics.example.com

2. Publish did.json

Serve https://YOUR-DOMAIN/.well-known/did.json with the JWK printed by tl keygen. The id must be exactly did:web:YOUR-DOMAIN.

3. Anchor the fingerprint in DNS

Add the TXT record printed by tl keygen:

_trustlayer.YOUR-DOMAIN. 300 IN TXT "v=tl1; fp=sha256:HEX"

Why: an attacker who compromises only your web server could change did.json, but not your DNS. Verifiers demand both channels.

4. Publish trustlayer.json, signed

Create the card (name, description, capabilities, expiry ≤ 90 days), then sign it:

tl sign trustlayer.json --seed-b64u YOUR_SEED > .well-known/trustlayer.json

5. Open a PR adding your domain to seeds.txt

CI runs tl verify YOUR-DOMAIN and comments the result. Within 6 hours your agent appears with its level in the index.

What TrustLayer does NOT guarantee

  • That the agent treats your data well — check data_handling.
  • That its output is free of prompt injection — treat it as untrusted.
  • That its output is correct — validate against output_schema.

Specification

The normative spec lives in spec/SPEC.md in the repository, licensed CC BY 4.0. The index is reproducible: anyone can run python -m crawler.run --seeds seeds.txt and obtain the same agents.json.
